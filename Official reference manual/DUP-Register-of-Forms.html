The Forms, Explained

Entries follow the usual path from Intake to Final Dispatch. Each description is based on what the simulator actually requires and records.

F-001
MAIN APPROVAL

Initial Petition & Numbering

ISSUING AUTHORITY Office of Initial Requests
REQUIRED PRIOR DOCUMENTATION

None

Registers the tiny undertaking, assigns its petition number, and verifies that an applicant has actually requested something.

Procedural effect. Allows Registry to examine F-008.

TYPICAL LEDGER EVIDENCE

“Intake stamps F-001 APPROVED.”

F-008
MAIN APPROVAL

Record of Prior Intention

ISSUING AUTHORITY Archive of Prior Intentions
REQUIRED PRIOR DOCUMENTATION

F-001

Establishes precedent: whether this act has already been contemplated, including whether the intention was previously intended.

Procedural effect. Unlocks the Safety and Decorum examinations. Under circular mandates, Registry also requires Dispatch (F-099), which cannot ordinarily approve until Registry has enabled the intermediate offices.

TYPICAL LEDGER EVIDENCE

“Registry holds F-008: awaiting Intake + Dispatch.”

F-012
MAIN APPROVAL

Certificate of Safety & Innocence

ISSUING AUTHORITY Bureau of Safety & Innocence
REQUIRED PRIOR DOCUMENTATION

F-008

Certifies that the proposed act will cause no measurable disturbance, including the absence of demonstrable risk.

Procedural effect. Required by Fiscal and Final Dispatch. Under circular mandates, Safety also requires Decorum (F-023), while Decorum requires Safety.

TYPICAL LEDGER EVIDENCE

“Safety stamps F-012 APPROVED.”

F-023
MAIN APPROVAL

Preliminary Decorum Assessment

ISSUING AUTHORITY Department of Decorum
REQUIRED PRIOR DOCUMENTATION

F-008

Determines whether the original proposed act is sufficiently appropriate and dignified to be considered.

Procedural effect. Required for Silly Walks (F-041) and Committee approval (F-047). Under circular mandates, Decorum and Safety require each other. This is not the later gait review F-023-G.

TYPICAL LEDGER EVIDENCE

“Decorum stamps F-023 APPROVED.”

F-034
MAIN APPROVAL

Fiscal Implications Certificate

ISSUING AUTHORITY Office of Fiscal Implications
REQUIRED PRIOR DOCUMENTATION

F-012

Approves the anticipated cost of the trivial act, including the cost of calculating that cost, to an absurd degree of precision.

Procedural effect. Required before the walking paperwork and Committee review may proceed.

TYPICAL LEDGER EVIDENCE

“Fiscal stamps F-034 APPROVED.”

F-041
MAIN APPROVAL

Certificate of Ceremonial Gait

ISSUING AUTHORITY Department of Silly Walks
REQUIRED PRIOR DOCUMENTATION

F-023 + F-034; also F-047 under circular mandates

Records completion of three officially unnecessary steps. The walk requires a provisional Committee permit (F-046-W) during its examination.

Procedural effect. Required by the Committee for its final decision. If rescinded or voided, the gait and its paperwork must be undertaken again. Circular mandates make Silly Walks demand final Committee approval before the walk, while the Committee demands F-041 first.

TYPICAL LEDGER EVIDENCE

“CEREMONIAL GAIT COMPLETED” followed by “Silly Walks stamps F-041 APPROVED.”

F-046-W
SUPPLEMENTARY PERMIT

Provisional Walking Permit

ISSUING AUTHORITY Committee on Minor Acts
REQUIRED PRIOR DOCUMENTATION

The F-041 process has begun

Authorizes the applicant to undertake an unnecessarily elaborate gait before the Committee has finally approved the petition.

Procedural effect. Permits the ceremonial gait to be completed and certified as F-041. It is a supporting permit, not a ninth main approval. It can be withdrawn following a Decorum veto.

TYPICAL LEDGER EVIDENCE

“PROVISIONAL WALK PERMIT: Committee on Minor Acts approves F-046-W.”

F-047
MAIN APPROVAL

Committee Resolution on a Minor Act

ISSUING AUTHORITY Committee on Minor Acts
REQUIRED PRIOR DOCUMENTATION

F-023 + F-034 + F-041

Records the Committee’s final assent to the minor act after the required gait has been certified.

Procedural effect. Normally opens the path to Dispatch. If Decorum’s Final Say is enabled, F-023-G must first resolve successfully; a veto annuls F-047, F-041 and F-046-W.

TYPICAL LEDGER EVIDENCE

“Committee stamps F-047 APPROVED.”

F-023-G
SUPPLEMENTARY REVIEW

Post-Committee Gait Conduct Review

ISSUING AUTHORITY Department of Decorum
REQUIRED PRIOR DOCUMENTATION

Approved F-041 and F-047, with Decorum’s Final Say enabled

Re-examines the conduct of an already approved silly walk. Decorum may affirm it or overrule the Committee on grounds of improper propriety.

Procedural effect. An affirmative finding releases Dispatch; a veto cancels the provisional permit F-046-W and the approvals F-041 and F-047, demanding a fresh walk and new Committee paperwork. It is not counted as an additional chamber signature.

TYPICAL LEDGER EVIDENCE

“DECORUM APPEAL OPENED” / “DECORUM AFFIRMS F-023-G” / “DECORUM VETO — F-023-G”.

F-099
MAIN APPROVAL

Final Seal & Dispatch Order

ISSUING AUTHORITY Final Seal & Dispatch
REQUIRED PRIOR DOCUMENTATION

F-047 + F-012; plus successful F-023-G when required

Confirms that all necessary approvals remain valid and grants technical permission to perform the requested trivial action.

Procedural effect. Completes authorization, not the act itself. The applicant still has to use the action button to ring the bell or perform the approved undertaking.

TYPICAL LEDGER EVIDENCE

“Dispatch stamps F-099 APPROVED.” / “FINAL AUTHORIZATION…”

How to Interpret a Ledger Entry

BEGINS EXAMINATION
The office has started reviewing the form; no approval has yet been issued.
HOLDS / AWAITING
A prerequisite stamp or conduct verdict is missing.
APPROVED
A current stamp exists. It can be challenged later.
RESCINDED / AUDITED / MISFILED
Evidence becomes invalid or unavailable. Later approvals may fall with it.
EXTRAORDINARY DISPENSATION
Ordinary evidence is waived; the office counts as satisfied without normal processing.
FORMAL CONTRADICTION
One or more circular prerequisite chains. Loop counts are not counts of separate forms.
FINAL AUTHORIZATION
Dispatch has authorized the act; the act itself may still be outstanding.
AUTHORIZED ACT EXECUTED
The applicant has carried out the insignificant deed.

The Official Silly-Walk Chain

  1. Decorum’s ordinary review F-023 and Fiscal’s certificate F-034 must be available.
  2. Silly Walks begins F-041; the Committee grants provisional gait permit F-046-W.
  3. Three unnecessary steps are performed and certified under F-041.
  4. The Committee gives final assent under F-047.
  5. If “Decorum’s Final Say” is enabled, F-023-G reviews the approved walk. A veto voids the permit, the gait certificate, and the Committee’s approval; the walk must be done again.
  6. Only after the required approvals and conduct review are valid may F-099 authorize the trivial act.
The Terminal paradox: Circular mandates make F-041 require F-047 while F-047 still requires F-041. Other circular mandates entangle Registry with Dispatch and Safety with Decorum. Waiting alone cannot resolve these loops.

A narrow loophole: The app’s special post-Committee conduct review is triggered by ordinary, approved F-041 and F-047 forms and an unwaived F-023. Extraordinary dispensations may therefore bypass an actual walk or its appeal.
